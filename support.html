Help with the Stylish Keyboard app for iPhone and iPad, and how to reach us.

GETTING STARTED

  • Add the keyboard: open iPhone Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard, and choose Stylish Keyboard. The app's setup walks you through it.
  • Switch to it: in any text field, touch and hold the globe key (🌐) and choose Stylish Keyboard. On iPhones with Face ID, the globe key is below the keyboard.
  • Choose your languages, theme, keyboard buddy and typing animations in the app.

FULL ACCESS (OPTIONAL)

The keyboard works fully without Full Access: typing, suggestions, corrections, emoji, themes and fonts. Full Access only turns on extras: the clipboard and Edit text panels, Recent copies, stickers, location sharing, key sounds and haptic feedback.

To turn it on or off: iPhone Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Stylish Keyboard → Allow Full Access.

iOS shows a strong warning for every keyboard that asks for Full Access. Stylish Keyboard never sends what you type anywhere, with or without it. Our privacy policy explains everything: https://shubhamroute.github.io/Stylish-keyboard-iOS-privacy-policy/

COMMON QUESTIONS

Why did the keyboard switch back to Apple's keyboard?

In password fields, iOS always uses its own keyboard, and some apps don't allow other keyboards at all. Switch back with the globe key (🌐) in any other field.

Why don't stickers paste?

Stickers need Full Access. Tap a sticker in the keyboard's Stickers panel, then touch and hold the message box and tap Paste. Some apps paste pictures as photos.

Why is the clipboard panel empty?

The clipboard panel and Recent copies need Full Access. Copy some text in any app, then open the clipboard panel. Recent copies keeps texts you copied for 24 hours, on your device; you can turn it off or clear it in the panel or in the app (Settings → Recent copies).

Why don't I hear key sounds or feel haptics?

Both need Full Access. Key sounds also need the keyboard sound on in iPhone Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Keyboard Feedback, and the switches in the app (Settings → Key sounds, Haptic feedback).

Why doesn't location sharing work?

Location sharing needs Full Access and iOS's location permission for Stylish Keyboard. In the keyboard, tap ☰ → Location; iOS asks for permission the first time. You can also allow it in the app's Location screen.

How do I type Kannada, Tamil or another language in English letters?

In the app, open Languages and turn on English (India) and your language (for example Kannada). The app downloads that language's word list, and English (India) suggests its words from the next time the keyboard opens.

How do I swipe type?

Slide your finger across the letters of a word without lifting it, then lift: the word goes in, and the next swiped word gets its space by itself. If it guessed wrong, tap the right word in the bar above the keys; delete right after a swiped word removes the whole word. It works in English, Hinglish and other languages typed in Latin letters. You can turn it off in the app: Settings → Swipe typing.

How do I move the cursor?

Slide your finger left or right on the space bar: the cursor follows, one letter at a time. A quick tap still types a space. You can turn it off in the app: Settings → Space bar cursor.

How do I hide the keyboard?

Tap the last button in the keyboard's toolbar (the keyboard with a down arrow). On iPad, the keyboard's own hide key works too.

How do I remove the words the keyboard learned?

In the app: Settings → Learned words → Clear learned words. You can also turn off Settings → Learn new words.

How do I see the setup again?

In the app: Settings → Show the setup again.

How do I delete my data?

Everything the app and keyboard keep stays on your device. Delete what you like in the app (Recent copies, My details, Quick replies, your personal dictionary, learned words, your photo theme), or delete the app to remove all of it. Usage statistics and crash reports can be turned off in Settings → Privacy.
